# AI Multi-Mind SaaS Engine - Launch Strategy

## Phase 1: The Beta Launch (Weeks 1-4)
- **Target**: 50 Beta Testers from niche business communities (IndieHackers, ProductHunt).
- **Offer**: Lifetime Free access to the "Starter" plan in exchange for feedback.
- **Goal**: Identify bugs, refine Mind prompts, and gather testimonials.

## Phase 2: The "Elite Access" Early Bird (Weeks 5-8)
- **Target**: Email list and social media followers.
- **Offer**: 50% off for life for the first 100 subscribers.
- **Goal**: Initial revenue and validation of pricing tiers.

## Phase 3: Public Launch (Week 9+)
- **ProductHunt Launch**: Coordinated push for "Product of the Day".
- **Affiliate Program**: Reach out to business influencers (Hormozi/Brunson fans) to promote via a 40% recurring commission.
- **Content Marketing**: Series of "How the CEO Mind solved X" case studies on LinkedIn/X.

## Launch Assets Required
- High-converting landing page (WP + Elementor/Oxygen).
- "Demo" video showing a 30-second Business Plan generation.
- Affiliate dashboard for partners.
