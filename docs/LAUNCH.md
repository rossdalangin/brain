# 🚀 AI Multi-Mind SaaS Engine - The "Grand Opening" Blitz

## ⚡ The Launch Philosophy
A product this powerful doesn't "launch"—it **ignites**. We follow the Brunson "Big Domino" theory: If we can convince the market that the "Council of Minds" is the only way to scale a business without hiring a $500k executive team, every other objection disappears.

## 📅 The 3-Phase Ignition Sequence

### Phase 1: The "Inner Circle" Beta (Weeks 1-3)
-   **The Hook**: "Be the Architect of the Future."
-   **Target**: 100 hand-picked Beta Testers from elite business masterminds.
-   **The Offer**: Lifetime access to the "Founder's Tier" in exchange for a video testimonial.
-   **Goal**: Create a wall of social proof before the public sees the price.

### Phase 2: The "Big Domino" Pre-Launch (Weeks 4-5)
-   **The Hook**: "Why your human team is failing you (and how the Council saves you)."
-   **Strategy**: A 3-part "Value Video" series (Hormozi style) demonstrating the engine solving impossible business problems in seconds.
-   **The Waitlist**: Collect 5,000+ emails using a viral referral loop ("Move up the line by inviting 3 friends").

### Phase 3: The Public Explosion (Week 6+)
-   **ProductHunt Blitz**: Aim for #1 Product of the Day.
-   **The "Price Anchor" Reveal**: Announce the $199/mo Agency plan, then offer a "Launch Week Special" for $99/mo.
-   **Affiliate Army**: Activate our partners with 40% recurring commissions, turning them into a 24/7 sales force.
