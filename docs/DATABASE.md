# 🔐 AI Multi-Mind SaaS Engine - The Data Vault (Schema)

## 🏛️ Infrastructure Philosophy
Our database is not just a collection of tables; it is the **ledger of your business**. Designed for high-concurrency and financial-grade accuracy, this schema ensures that credits are never "lost" and subscriptions are never "leaked."

## 💎 Custom SaaS Tables (High-Performance Layer)

### 1. `wp_amm_subscriptions`
The core of your recurring revenue.
- `id`: Primary identity.
- `user_id`: Link to the WP User.
- `plan_id`: (free, starter, pro, agency) - The tier of access.
- `gateway`: (stripe, paypal) - Gateway redundancy.
- `subscription_id`: Gateway-specific pointer.
- `status`: Financial state (active, trialing, past_due, cancelled).
- `current_period_end`: Precise expiration timestamp.

### 2. `wp_amm_usage` (The Metering Hub)
This table protects your margins.
- `id`: Identity.
- `user_id`: Link.
- `month`: (YYYY-MM) Partitioned usage tracking.
- `credits_used`: Total consumption of AI intelligence.
- `last_reset`: When the "Credit Tank" was last refilled.

### 3. `wp_amm_teams` & `wp_amm_team_members`
The Collaboration Engine.
- Manages hierarchies, shared workspace ownership, and granular permissions for Agency-level deployments.

### 4. `wp_amm_affiliates` & `wp_amm_referrals`
The Viral Growth Engine.
- Tracks the performance of your advocates and the dividends they earn.

## 📦 WordPress Integration Layer
- **`ai_minds` (CPT)**: Strategic persona definitions.
- **`ai_outputs` (CPT)**: The high-value assets generated by the engine.
- **`ai_templates` (CPT)**: Pre-packaged strategic frameworks.

## 🛡️ Security Measures
- **AES-256 Encryption**: Used for all external API keys at rest.
- **Audit Logging**: Every credit transaction is logged in the `amm-logs` directory for manual reconciliation if needed.
