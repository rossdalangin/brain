# 10-Step Success Blueprint: Start Here 🚀

Welcome to the Council. Follow these steps to generate your first billion-dollar strategy.

1. **Brand Profile**: Enter the Onboarding Wizard and define your business. The more specific you are, the better the AI thinks.
2. **The Target Persona**: Go to the 'Audience' tab. Define exactly who you are helping. AI Minds use this context to customize every word.
3. **Select Your Mind**: Browse the 'Minds Library'. Need an offer? Pick the **Offer Creator**. Need a funnel? Pick the **Funnel Builder**.
4. **Ignite the Engine**: Paste your context into the 'Ignite Mind' tab. Watch the simulated thinking build your strategy in real-time.
5. **Summon the Council**: For complex tasks, use the 'Mind Council'. Try **Critique Mode** to have a Strategist audit your Copywriter's work.
6. **Context Pro**: Got a client CSV or a data report? Upload it in 'Settings'. The engine will perform a RAG analysis on your private data.
7. **Refine with Magic BFF**: Use the refinement button to have the engine self-correct and improve its own prompts.
8. **Save to Workspace**: All your strategies are saved automatically. Organize them into folders (e.g., "Client A", "Q4 Launch").
9. **Export the Blueprint**: Select your best outputs and click 'Export Blueprint' to create a single Markdown or PDF document.
10. **Team Hub**: Invite your team. Scale your agency by sharing intelligence and delegating the "Ignite" process.
