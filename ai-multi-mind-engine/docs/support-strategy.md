# Billion-Dollar Customer Support & Retention

Retention is the bedrock of SaaS valuation. A $1B SaaS typically has a net revenue retention (NRR) of 110%+.

## 1. AI-Powered Support (Deflection)
- **Strategy**: Train a custom "Support Mind" on your own documentation and the `audit_trail` (anonymized) to resolve 80% of user queries before they reach a human.
- **Implementation**: Embed a specialized chatbot in the Dashboard that handles account, billing, and "How to prompt" questions.

## 2. Proactive "Value" Injection
- **Churn Signals**: Monitor usage trends. If a user's credits drop by 50% month-over-month, trigger an automated "Magic BFF" check-in.
- **Hook**: "Hey, I noticed you haven't ignited the Council lately. Here is a new $100M Offer template I built for your industry."

## 3. The Community Moat
- **Concept**: User-generated custom minds. Allow high-tier users to "Showcase" their custom minds in a private community forum.
- **Lock-in**: When users build their "Thinking Frameworks" into your platform, the cost of switching to a generic LLM becomes infinite.

## 4. Dunning & Financial Stability
- **Smart Retries**: Use Stripe's automated retry logic combined with a personalized "Account Specialist" email (automated) after the 2nd failed attempt.
- **Downgrade Path**: Instead of cancellation, offer a "Parked" plan for $5/mo that preserves their conversation history and saved strategies.

## 5. Net Promoter Score (NPS) Loops
- **Mechanism**: Every generation has a 👍/👎. Use this to identify "Promoters" for the Affiliate program and "Detractors" for immediate success outreach.
