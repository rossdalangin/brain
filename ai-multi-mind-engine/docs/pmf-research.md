# Product-Market Fit & Persona Research

The AI Multi-Mind Engine is designed for three primary personas who feel the "pain" of strategic indecision or high consulting costs.

## Persona 1: "The Solopreneur Sarah"
- **Pain Point**: Overwhelmed by too many tasks; lacks a strategic partner to bounce ideas off.
- **Killer Feature**: **Magic BFF**. Sarah uses it as a daily business partner who remembers her goals.
- **Willingness to Pay**: $19 - $49/mo.

## Persona 2: "The Agency Owner Alex"
- **Pain Point**: High overhead costs for human strategists; slow turnaround for client deliverables (SOPs, Ad Copy).
- **Killer Feature**: **Team Hub & White-Labeling**. Alex uses the tool as a "proprietary" value-add for his clients.
- **Willingness to Pay**: $199 - $499/mo.

## Persona 3: "The Corporate Intrapreneur Chris"
- **Pain Point**: Bureaucratic inertia; needs to quickly draft high-quality business cases or risk assessments for management.
- **Killer Feature**: **The Council**. Chris uses sequential logic to build professional-grade reports that look like they came from a Tier-1 consulting firm.
- **Willingness to Pay**: $99/mo (Enterprise expense account).

---

## PMF Signals (Metrics to Track)
- **Time to First Ignition**: Goal is < 3 minutes after signup.
- **Council Adoption**: Percentage of users using multi-mind sequences vs. single mind prompts.
- **Viral Coefficient**: Number of new signups coming from 'Shared Intelligence' public links.
