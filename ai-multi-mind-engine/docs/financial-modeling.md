# Financial Modeling & Unit Economics

To scale the AI Multi-Mind Engine to a billion-dollar valuation, we must optimize the core SaaS metrics: LTV, CAC, and MRR.

## 1. Unit Economics (Per User)
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**:
  - *Target*: $30 - $60 via Affiliate Network (30% recurring commission).
  - *Viral Loop*: Shared Intelligence links provide "Free" organic acquisition.
- **LTV (Lifetime Value)**:
  - *Target*: $450+ (Average retention of 13 months at $35 ARPU).
- **Payback Period**: Goal is to recoup CAC within 2 months of subscription.

## 3. Margin Optimization (COGS)
LLM API costs are our primary variable cost.
- **Gemini Pro**: $0 (Free Tier) - Used for 70% of "Core" tasks.
- **GPT-4 Turbo**: $0.03 / 1k tokens - Used for "Council" and "Elite" Minds.
- **Strategy**: By routing simple requests to Gemini and complex frameworks to GPT-4, we maintain a **Gross Margin of 85%+**.

## 4. Churn Mitigation
- **The "BFF" Effect**: Personalized AI memory creates a high switching cost.
- **Agency Lock-in**: Once an agency onboards 10 clients/team members, the cost of migration becomes prohibitive.
- **Quarterly Plans**: Offer 20% discount on annual billing to secure long-term cash flow and reduce monthly churn.
