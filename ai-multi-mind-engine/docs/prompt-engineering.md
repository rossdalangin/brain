# Advanced Prompt Engineering: The Hidden Layer

The power of the AI Multi-Mind SaaS Engine lies in its "Hidden Prompt Engineering Layer." This document explains how we transform generic AI into elite business minds.

## 1. Cognitive Framing
Every mind is "pre-framed" before the user even types a word. We use high-authority persona anchoring.
- **Example**: Instead of "You are a CEO," we use "You are an elite Fortune 500 CEO with 25 years of experience in multi-variable optimization and lean operations."

## 2. Multi-Step Chain of Thought (CoT)
Our "Council" feature leverages CoT by passing the output of one mind as the input to the next.
- **Sequence**: CEO (Strategy) -> Funnel Architect (Implementation) -> Copywriter (Messaging).

## 3. Framework Anchoring
Generic AI tends to give generic advice. We force the AI to anchor its responses in proven business frameworks.
- **Hormozi Framework**: We hardcode the Value Equation into the system prompt.
- **Brunson Framework**: We hardcode the "Hook-Story-Offer" structure.

## 4. Constraint-Based Output
Elite minds don't just give answers; they work within constraints. We instruct our AI minds to consider:
- **Unit Economics**: Is the suggested strategy profitable?
- **Resource Constraints**: Can a small team actually execute this?
- **Time Delay**: How fast will this yield an ROI?

## 5. The "BFF" Memory Layer
By injecting recent user history into the prompt, we create a "Mental Model" of the user's business. This allows the AI to give contextually relevant advice that "feels" like it knows the company.
