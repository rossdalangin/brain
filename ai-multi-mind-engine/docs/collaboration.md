# Team Collaboration & Elite Workspace

## Team Hub (Agency Feature)
The Agency Plan unlocks the Team Hub, allowing owners to invite members and manage their permissions.

### Roles & Permissions
- **Admin (Owner)**: Full access to billing, settings, and team management.
- **Member**: Can generate insights, view shared workspace, and contribute to the Council.
- **Client (Custom)**: View-only access to specific folders (Headless Roadmap).

### White-Labeling
Agency owners can upload their own logo and set primary brand colors. This transforms the dashboard into a proprietary tool for their own clients.

## The Workspace
Every generation is automatically saved and indexed.

### Organization
- **Folders**: Users can create custom folders (e.g., "Project X", "Client Y") to organize outputs.
- **Search**: Instant full-text search across all saved strategies.
- **Versioning**: Users can duplicate an output to create a "v2" with different AI instructions.

### Export & Sharing
- **PDF/HTML**: One-click export for professional presentation.
- **Shared Intel**: Generate a public, unindexed link to share a specific strategy with a client or partner without granting dashboard access.
