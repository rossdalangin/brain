# SaaS Unit Economics: The Advanced Edition 📈

Building a billion-dollar SaaS requires more than just revenue; it requires **efficient growth**. This guide covers the advanced metrics used by elite VCs and CEOs.

## 1. Magic Number (Sales Efficiency)
The "Magic Number" measures how much revenue you generate for every dollar spent on Sales and Marketing.
- **Formula**: `(Current Quarter Revenue - Previous Quarter Revenue) * 4 / (Previous Quarter S&M Spend)`
- **Benchmark**: > 0.75 is good. > 1.0 is world-class.

## 2. Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
The most important metric for multi-billion dollar exits. It measures how much your existing customer base grows without adding new customers.
- **Formula**: `(Starting MRR + Expansion MRR - Churned MRR - Contraction MRR) / Starting MRR`
- **Benchmark**: 120%+ is the goal for enterprise SaaS.

## 3. Burn Multiple
Measures how much cash you are burning relative to how much Net New ARR you are adding.
- **Formula**: `Net Burn / Net New ARR`
- **Benchmark**:
  - < 1.0: Amazing
  - 1.0 - 1.5: Good
  - > 2.0: Problematic

## 4. Cohort LTV (Predictive)
Instead of a simple average LTV, use cohort-based LTV to see if your product improvements are actually increasing the value of newer customers.
- **Strategy**: Compare the 12-month value of customers acquired in Q1 vs Q3. If Q3 is higher, your product-market fit (PMF) is strengthening.

## 5. Payback Period by Channel
Never look at a blended CAC. Break down payback periods by source:
- **Affiliate Channel**: Low CAC, high volume (Target: 3-month payback).
- **Direct Sales**: High CAC, high LTV (Target: 12-month payback).
- **Organic Content**: $0 CAC, long-term asset.

## 6. The "SaaS Death Cross"
This occurs when your Churn MRR exceeds your New MRR.
- **Solution**: Focus on "Product-Led Growth" (PLG) and "Success Coaching" (using our AI Success Coach) to ensure users are actually getting value from their credits.
