# Customer Success & Onboarding

A SaaS lives or dies by its first 5 minutes of user experience. The AI Multi-Mind Engine is designed to provide immediate "Aha!" moments.

## 1. The Onboarding Tour
- **Implementation**: The dashboard includes an "Onboarding Overlay" that guides the user through their first "Ignition."
- **First-Day Goal**: Get the user to generate at least one high-value output (e.g., a Sales Script) within 3 minutes of signing up.

## 2. Milestone Engagement
- **10 Generations**: Send a "Strategic Master" badge and a 10% discount on a Premium Mind unlock.
- **Team Invite**: Offer a "Collaboration Pro" guide explaining how to use the Council effectively with clients.

## 3. Churn Prevention (Dunning)
- **Failed Payments**: Automated Stripe emails with a direct link to the **Billing Portal**.
- **Usage Thresholds**: The "90% Credit Alert" (implemented in `AMM_Usage_Tracker`) prompts users to upgrade to a higher tier before they hit a wall.

## 4. The "Strategy Audit" Feedback Loop
- Encourage users to use the **"Critique Mode"** in the Council. When they see a second AI mind "fix" the first one's strategy, the perceived value of the platform triples.

## 5. Success Management
- For Agency users, provide monthly "Success Strategy" reports showing how many hours of human labor their AI council has replaced.
- **Metric**: 1 AI Generation = 2 Hours of Strategic Consulting saved.
