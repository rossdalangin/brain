# Affiliate Program Strategy: Recruitment & Scaling

The Affiliate Network is our primary growth lever. By recruiting high-authority business influencers, we can scale MRR with a fixed acquisition cost.

## 1. Recruitment Strategy
- **Target Profiles**: Business coaches, SaaS influencers, YouTubers in the "Make Money Online" niche, and Marketing agency owners.
- **The "High-Ticket" Bridge**: Position the AI Multi-Mind Engine as the tool coaches give their clients to *implement* their teachings.
- **Outreach**: Offer top-tier affiliates a "Founding Partner" badge and a temporary commission bump to 40%.

## 2. Commission Structure
- **Standard**: 30% Monthly Recurring Commission for the life of the customer.
- **Pro**: 40% for affiliates who refer 50+ paid users.
- **Payouts**: Monthly net-30 via PayPal or Bank Transfer, verified against Stripe renewal status.

## 3. Affiliate Enablement Kit
- **Swipe Files**: Pre-written email sequences and social media posts.
- **Demo Assets**: Short video clips of the "Council" solving complex business problems.
- **Tracking**: Built-in Dashboard tab showing real-time clicks, referrals, and earnings.

## 4. Viral "White-Label" Hook
- Encourage Agency-level affiliates to use their own branding on the platform. When they show their clients "their" proprietary AI tool, they are effectively selling your SaaS for you.
