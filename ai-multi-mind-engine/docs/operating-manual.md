# SaaS Operating Manual: Billion-Dollar Efficiency

This manual details how to operate the AI Multi-Mind Engine as a high-margin, low-overhead business.

## 1. Minimalist Staffing
- **Product & Tech**: 1 Senior Architect (WordPress/React) and 1 Prompt Engineer.
- **Growth**: 1 Affiliate Manager to recruit and manage top 1% business influencers.
- **Support**: 1 Customer Success Lead (augmented by the "Support Mind" AI).
- **Outsourcing**: Use high-tier freelancers for specialized UI/UX or deep security audits.

## 2. API Cost Management
- **Quotas**: Stick to the credit limits defined in the `Usage Tracker`.
- **Free Jules**: Keep the mock fallback updated with high-value generic tips to maintain user trust even during API outages.
- **Token Optimization**: Use the "Magic Prompt Refiner" to condense user input before sending it to expensive models like GPT-4.

## 3. Weekly Revenue Rhythms
- **Monday**: Review MRR and Churn stats in Admin Analytics.
- **Wednesday**: Reach out to top 10 "Promoters" (from NPS thumbs-up data).
- **Friday**: Launch a "Featured Mind" or "Template Pack" to drive expansion revenue.

## 4. Disaster Recovery
- **Backups**: Ensure daily database backups are stored off-site. The `wp_amm_usage` and `wp_amm_subscriptions` tables are mission-critical.
- **API Redundancy**: If OpenAI is down, toggle the default provider to Gemini in the Admin Settings.

## 5. Visionary Leadership
- Never stop being the **Billionaire Strategist**. Focus on the "Operating System for Business Thought" positioning. Don't build just another AI tool—build the future of decision-making.
