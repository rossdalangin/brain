# Marketing & Growth Funnels

## 1. The "Elite Insights" Lead Magnet
- **Front-end Funnel**: A free tool that allows 1 generation without registration.
- **Hook**: "Get an Elite CEO to audit your business in 30 seconds."
- **Conversion**: Prompt for email to "Unlock the full 10-page strategy."

## 2. Viral Shared Intelligence
- Every generated output has a "Share" button.
- Shared links point to a beautifully formatted, public page with a persistent header:
  - *"This strategy was built by the AI Multi-Mind Engine. [Get Your Own Minds]"*
- This creates a viral loop as users share their impressive results on LinkedIn and Twitter.

## 3. The Agency Multiplier
- By offering "Team Hub" and "White-Labeling," we encourage consultants to bring their entire client base onto the platform.
- Every client added is a potential future direct subscriber.

## 4. Affiliate "Engine Rooms"
- Influencers can create their own "Custom Minds" and templates, then promote the platform using their referral link.
- They act as "Curators" of elite minds, earning 30% recurring commission.

## 5. Performance Marketing (Ads)
- **YouTube**: Demo videos of "The $100M Offer Creator" building a product in real-time.
- **Facebook**: Comparison ads: "Traditional Consultant ($5,000) vs. AI Multi-Mind ($49)."
