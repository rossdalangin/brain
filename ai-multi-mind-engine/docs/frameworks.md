# Elite Business Frameworks

The AI Multi-Mind Engine is powered by the world's most successful business frameworks. Every AI Mind is hardcoded to think within these specific cognitive structures.

## 1. The $100M Offer Framework (Alex Hormozi)
- **Used by**: *Offer Creator*, *Pricing Strategist*.
- **Core Logic**: Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement / Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice.
- **Goal**: Create an "irresistible" offer where the value is so high that price becomes irrelevant.

## 2. The Funnel Hacking Framework (Russell Brunson)
- **Used by**: *Funnel Architect*, *Copywriting Master*.
- **Core Logic**: Hook -> Story -> Offer.
- **Goal**: Guide a prospect through a sequence of value levels, maximizing the average order value (AOV).

## 3. Blue Ocean Strategy (Chan Kim & Renée Mauborgne)
- **Used by**: *Elite Strategist*, *Market Dominator*.
- **Core Logic**: Value Innovation. Don't compete in the "Red Ocean" of existing markets; create a "Blue Ocean" of uncontested market space.
- **Goal**: Make the competition irrelevant by differentiating and lowering costs.

## 4. The Theory of Constraints (Eliyahu Goldratt)
- **Used by**: *Systems Builder*, *SOP Architect*.
- **Core Logic**: A chain is only as strong as its weakest link. Identify the bottleneck and exploit it.
- **Goal**: Maximize throughput and efficiency in business operations.

## 5. Jobs to be Done (Clayton Christensen)
- **Used by**: *Product Strategist*, *UX Expert*.
- **Core Logic**: People don't "buy" products; they "hire" them to do a job.
- **Goal**: Align product features with the actual emotional and functional progress the user is trying to make.

## 6. Extreme Ownership (Jocko Willink)
- **Used by**: *Leadership Coach*.
- **Core Logic**: There are no bad teams, only bad leaders. Total responsibility for every outcome.
- **Goal**: Build high-performance, decentralized command structures.
